    Inside the model

    Every sentence of your text. Brighter = higher TF-IDF score; underlined sentences made the summary. Hover for the exact score.

    How it works

    The same steps as summarize.py — no neural network, fully explainable.

    1. Split the text into sentences at . ! and ?
    2. Weigh words with TF-IDF: words that are frequent in one sentence but rare overall score high.
    3. Score sentences by adding up their word weights.
    4. Keep the top N and put them back in reading order.

    Python version: python summarize.py my.txt --sentences 5. Both versions pick the same sentences.
