    How it works

    1. Split the text into sentences at . ! and ?
    2. Weigh words with TF-IDF — frequent in one sentence, rare overall, scores high.
    3. Score sentences by adding up their word weights.
    4. Keep the top N and put them back in reading order.

    Same steps as summarize.py — no neural network, fully explainable. Python: python summarize.py my.txt --sentences 5
